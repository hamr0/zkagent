  intent, the already-handled `av://` link. The app captured it again as a pendingHandoff, re-fetched the request (the
  second GET), refused it again (15:42:12) and wrote a second Log-tab entry. Not a D112 defect; recorded as finding #48.
- Check 4: the orchestrator opened the age-18 lock-setting request before the owner said "go". The 5-minute TTL made it
  harmless; recorded honestly.
